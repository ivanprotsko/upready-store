# Wholesale

Cherkashina Oksana supplies natural health products directly from manufacturers to salons, studios, and retail outlets. We work with partners in Russia, Kazakhstan, and other CIS countries.

## What we offer partners

- Direct contracts with manufacturers of natural products and herbal supplements
- Wholesale prices — discount off retail on orders above a set amount
- Authenticity guarantee on all products
- Logistics: delivery across Russia and CIS (CDEK), Europe and USA (DHL)
- Marketing support: materials, training, certificates

1. Email info@cherkashina.ru or fill in the form below
2. Get access to the closed price list and catalogue
3. We will agree on the first order and shipping terms
