К примеру если мы договоримся с сервисами такими как Robokassa работать напрямую, чтобы мы становились гарантами, которые будут проверять магазины клиентов и к примеру получать ключ минуя все процедуры напрямую с клиентом. При этом нам даже не нужно брать какой-то процент, потому что все деньги будут напрямую идти например Robokassa. Думаю это эти материалы нужно уже начинать готовить изкчив документации, вопросы и ответы продукотов: робокасса, юкасса, сдек, почта россии, яндекс айди и добавить в офёрту что мы при желании клиента выполним определенную часть работ и отправим ему документы, и логины и пароли, которые он после того как всё заработает должен будет сменить»

## Goal
A decision document for Ivan plus a first onboarding kit, built from PRIMARY sources only (official sites, developer docs, FAQ/help centres, partner/agent/referral programme pages, published offers/contract templates) of five services: Robokassa, YooKassa (ЮKassa), CDEK, Почта России (business account / Otpravka), Yandex ID (OAuth app registration and moderation). ApiShip is our aggregator for CDEK — note where it changes the answer. Marketplace developer documentation (WB, Ozon) is out of scope and stays closed, as the preamble says.

## Questions to answer per service (each answer = citation: URL + verbatim quote + fetched-at, from raw/)
1. Partner / agent / reseller / referral / integrator programmes: may a SaaS platform register merchants on their behalf, act as technical integrator, obtain keys through a partner cabinet, white-label the onboarding? Requirements (legal entity, contract, volumes) and what their offer FORBIDS (credential sharing, acting as a payment intermediary). Legal questions are FLAGGED «требует юриста», never answered from memory (rule 17: no invented legal facts).
2. The merchant onboarding path as the client will live it: documents, verification steps, timing, the «1 ₽ payment to bind bank details» at Robokassa (verify it in their docs — if not found, write «не найдено в источниках»), the CDEK manager call, the Почта России account top-up, Yandex ID app moderation. Output: a step list per service, each step tagged «делает клиент» / «можем сделать мы» / «только клиент (2FA, паспорт, подпись)».
3. Money flow: confirm money goes PSP → merchant directly, we take no percent, and where a platform can stand as «technical partner» without touching funds (cite).
4. Credentials handover model: we register cabinets on the client's behalf → risks (2FA on the client's phone, KYC needs the client's documents, 152-ФЗ personal data, offer clauses, account ownership); a recommended model as OPTIONS with pros/cons (client registers by our step-by-step + screen-share · we register by power of attorney · partner cabinet issues keys) — the decision is Ivan's, do not make it for him.
5. Materials: per service a Russian text-instruction skeleton and a video-script outline (≤3 min, what is on screen) → `kits/<service>.md`; an offer clause DRAFT (ru): we perform part of the work on the client's request, hand over documents, logins and passwords, the client must change passwords after go-live — marked «ЧЕРНОВИК, требует юриста».
6. Backlog: `onboarding-tasks.json` (form of tasks.json, ids ob01…): for each task «что даёт клиенту», «что нужно от сервиса», «нужен ли договор/партнёрка», priority must/should/later.

## Economy with quality (rule 47)
Pages are fetched by a SCRIPT, not by the model: `scripts/fetch.mjs` — sequential, ≥2 s between requests, UA `upready-research/1.0 (+https://upready.dev)`, timeout 20 s, ≤40 pages per service, no retries on 403/429, no headed browsers, never Ivan's Chrome; text extracts to `raw/<service>/<slug>.md` with the header `# Source / # Fetched / # Topic / # HTTP` (reuse `artifacts/research/2026-09-16-store-templates-functionality/scripts/fetch.mjs` from dev if it is there after `git fetch`, otherwise write your own to the same rules). Extraction of facts per page — sonnet subagents on a JSON schema `{service, question(1..4), claim, quote, url}` with a gate in CODE (a claim without a verbatim quote found in raw/ is dropped and logged); synthesis and the kits — opus. report.md ends with `## Экономия`.
