# Owner Panel UX Audit, 2026-09-21

Task t-2026-09-21-02, order o-20260921-01. This audit is the "before" reference of the owner panel
redesign. It records what a shop owner with no technical background meets on every screen of
`apps/tenant-panel`, ranks the problems, and states the fix and the redesign plan per screen.

## How the Audit Was Made

The stand is a local copy of a real herbal shop: 38 products, 9 categories, 5 content pages, 4
delivery methods, 20 orders in five states, 10 customers, 8 reviews that wait for moderation, a
Wildberries key that the marketplace refuses and no Ozon key. The orders, customers and reviews were
created over HTTP by `apps/tenant-panel/e2e/seed-owner-stand.mjs`, through the same routes the
storefront and the panel use.

`apps/tenant-panel/e2e/owner-walker.e2e.mjs` logged in from the login screen and opened every route
of `apps/tenant-panel/e2e/owner-routes.mjs` at 1440 and at 390 pixels. Per screen it saved a
full-height screenshot and the `innerText`. The result is 104 screens per width, 0 crashed screens,
0 screens with document-level horizontal overflow and 12 unresolved routes.

Every frame was opened and looked at. The coverage has three honest limits.

- Byte-identical frames were detected with `md5` and looked at once: 11 of the 12 unbuilt order
  screens, the two Ozon routes, the two category ordering routes.
- At 1440 the 21 product overlays sit over the same 2025 pixel product page. The page was read in
  full once, and each overlay was read as a crop of its top 1000 pixels, where the overlay is.
- At 390 the frames were read as contact sheets of phone screens (844 CSS pixels each). Pages were
  read as their first two phone screens plus their last one, overlays as their first phone screen.
  The middle of a very tall page at 390 was therefore read at 1440 only.

The 12 unresolved routes need data the stand does not have yet: a product import, a product
attribute, a promotion, a campaign and a journal article. Their list screens and create forms were
walked, their detail and edit screens were not. Marketplace orders cannot be seeded over HTTP, so
`/marketplace-orders/:id` was opened with a shop order id, which is also what a row click on that
list does today.

One stand artefact affects the frames: 313 product images point at another local stand, so product
photos are broken in the panel. The real finding inside it is F21.

All screenshot paths below are absolute. Binaries stay out of git.

## What Stops the Owner From Finishing a Daily Job on Her Phone

The lead asked for this order first. The three daily jobs are an order taken to delivery, a product
with a photo and a price, and a page edit. The findings that stop each job at 390 pixels come first,
and the work of Milestone 2 follows this order after the two changes that touch every screen
(navigation P1 and plain language P3).

1. Order to delivery: F4 (packing may be blocked), F3 (no next action on the order), F2 (the orders
   list hides status, sum and delivery), F17 (menus lead to English dead ends).
2. Product with photo and price: F1 ("Создать" is off-screen on the products list), F7 (no visible
   price, a spreadsheet to change it, price on step three of the wizard), F11 (the creation dialog
   is squeezed to about 200 pixels).
3. Page edit: F10 (bare text box, no preview), F1 (clipped save button), F11 (no side padding).
4. Every job: F5 (menu), F6 (engineering words), F15 (inner overflow).

## Where the Audit Disagrees With the Decisions

The decisions are implemented as given. Four points are recorded for the lead.

1. "Маркетинг" is a group with one entry. A group with one child costs a click and an extra line. It
   is built as decided, and it opens its only screen on a click on the group title.
2. The hub "Площадки" lists five tabs. The panel also has "Цены и остатки" and "Аналитика" for
   marketplaces. "Цены и остатки" becomes a view inside the tab "Каталог", and "Аналитика" becomes a
   sixth tab, so that every route stays within two clicks.
3. "Настройки" lists seven entries. The panel also has search engine verification, analytics
   counters and old addresses. They become one entry "Поиск и счётчики" with three tabs. Branding is
   "Витрина → Оформление". Robokassa, CDEK and marketplace keys move into "Оплата", "Доставка" and
   "Площадки → Подключение".
4. Decision P4 applies the editor to the product description "if Markdown". The description is
   checked against the storefront in Milestone 2. If the storefront prints it as plain text, the
   editor is not applied there, because a toolbar that produces characters the shop page shows
   literally would be worse than a text box.
